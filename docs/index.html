About

مزرعتي (My Farm) is an offline-first farm management app designed for small and medium farms. Track animals, feed, purchases, sales, births, health, production, treasury, expenses, reports, and contacts — all with no account, no server, and no internet. Everything stays safely on your Android device.

Key Features

🐄 Animals

Manage herd by type, status, tags, breeds, ages, and lineage.

🌾 Feed & Stock

Track feed inventory, transactions, allocations, and low-stock alerts.

💰 Sales & Purchases

Record transactions with quantities, units, prices, totals, and payment status.

🏥 Health & Vet

Treatments, diagnoses, medicine/vet costs, visits, and per-animal history.

🍼 Births & Production

Track births and daily production with values and reports.

🏦 Treasury

Monitor balances, incomes, expenses, and financial summaries.

📊 Reports

Insights across animals, sales, purchases, feed, health, production, and expenses.

💾 Backup & Export

Export as JSON or Excel, import safely, and share files directly from the app.

🌍 Multi-language

Full Arabic, English, and French support with RTL/LTR awareness.

📱 Offline-first

On-device SQLite via @capacitor-community/sqlite — works without internet.
